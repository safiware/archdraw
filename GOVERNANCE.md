# Governance

archdraw is maintained by [Safiware](https://archdraw.dev). The maintainers decide what goes into the project, review and merge pull requests, and cut releases.

- **Proposals** for features and changes of direction are made in [Discussions](https://github.com/safiware/archdraw/discussions) or an issue. Maintainers explain their decisions there.
- **Releases** are tagged from `main` and built by CI; a maintainer reviews each draft release before publishing it.
- **Becoming a maintainer:** contributors whose work and reviews show good judgment over time are invited to help maintain the project.

This document will grow as the community does.
